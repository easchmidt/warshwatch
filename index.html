About this dashboard

At Jackson Hole, Warsh set out seven principles. The first was that policy should rest on data that are relevant, contemporaneous, accurate and actionable, and that trends matter most. This page collects the measures he cited and shows how they have moved since.

Method notes

  • Breadth. The share of individual PCE price components whose inflation rate is above 3%, over 12 months and over 6 months (annualized), with each component counted equally. Warsh cited 199 components from Federal Reserve staff work. This page rebuilds the measure from the most detailed lines of BEA's NIPA Table 2.4.4U (about 220 components), so levels differ slightly from the speech: 51% against 54% for July 2026, using today's revised data.
  • "At Jackson Hole" markers. These are the values Warsh quoted in the speech, which used data available in August 2026. Later data revisions can change the historical readings shown here.
  • Substitutes. National-accounts corporate profits stand in for S&P 500 earnings, and Treasury breakevens stand in for inflation swaps, because those series are not freely licensed.
  • Corporate bond spread history on FRED is limited to roughly the last three years.
  • October 2025 CPI was never published because of the government shutdown. Where a percent change needs a missing month as its base, that month is estimated as the geometric mean of the months on either side.
